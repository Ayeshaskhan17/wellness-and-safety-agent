The  Agent is a proactive, safety-critical system designed to support mental wellness by bridging the gap between individuals and professional help. Its primary purpose is to provide empathetic, vetted self-care resources while maintaining strict clinical boundaries. The agent will utilize RAG-based retrieval to securely offer mindfulness strategies and coping techniques for daily stress management. It continuously monitors conversations for high-risk distress signals to ensure early detection and proactive prevention. Crucially, the agent functions as a non-medical first responder, instantly routing users to official crisis helplines and human support when acute safety risks are identified
